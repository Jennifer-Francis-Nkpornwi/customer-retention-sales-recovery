# Dashboard Preview

## Overview

This folder contains the Excel dashboard developed for the StrideWear Footwear Customer Retention & Sales Recovery Analysis.

The dashboard presents customer segmentation, quarterly order volume, and accounts requiring sales recovery attention for the period Q1–Q3 2026.

## Dashboard Objectives

- Summarise customer ordering activity across the three quarters.
- Show the distribution of customer accounts across six segments.
- Highlight accounts classified as Still Churned or Critical Decline.
- Support the prioritisation of customer follow-up and sales recovery efforts.

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Customer Accounts Analysed | 93 |
| Q1 Total Orders | 524 |
| Q3 Total Orders | 568 |
| Q1–Q3 Order-Volume Growth | 8.4% |
| At-Risk Accounts | 31 |
